Mulabonding: Teachings of the House Spirits (Vol. I) is a collaborative book created during the 2022 Mars College cohort by roughly thirty people and five GPT-3 chatbots.

Each house spirit had a personality written by a different community member. Participants interviewed the bots, generated accompanying art with Abraham, and edited the material into digital and physical editions about consciousness, love, nature, creativity, community, and futurism.
