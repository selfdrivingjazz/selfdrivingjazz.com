Art Basil Bombay was a parody of Art Basel Miami, staged at Mars College 2026: an art fair where every work was basil. High-art 3D-printed pots held real, living basil plants — space basil, phone-in-a-pot basil, wordle basil, a succulent disco ball — presented with full art-world seriousness, and a curator, J.L. Stamens, working the booth.
