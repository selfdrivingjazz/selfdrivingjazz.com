Introverse is a card game for deep connection: a therapy session Trojan-horsed into a party game. Players move through six emotional archetypes and three levels of questions, games, and rituals.

The 120 activities draw on years of traveling, living in intentional communities, and learning what helps socially curious people move beyond small talk. It was designed in collaboration with Lucy.
